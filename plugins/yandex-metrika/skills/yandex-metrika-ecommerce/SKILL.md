# Analyze Metrika ecommerce

Read `../../references/reporting.md` and `../../references/attribution.md`.

## Workflow

Establish currency, date range, attribution and whether ecommerce data is sufficiently populated. Analyze orders, revenue, AOV and product performance separately from configured goals.

When performance changes, decompose by source/channel, campaign when available, device, landing/category/product mix and conversion rate before claiming a cause.

If ecommerce totals materially diverge from the business/order system, treat that as a measurement issue and quantify the gap before optimizing marketing from Metrika alone.

## PnL and advertising economics

A **PnL** / ROAS conclusion requires **compatible spend** and **revenue** evidence for the same period and a compatible attribution/time basis. Direct advertising cost and ecommerce revenue may need separate reports/scopes; join them only after verifying that the dimensions and period basis are comparable.

Keep Direct **clicks**, Metrika **visits**, goal reaches and ecommerce transactions distinct. A click is not a visit and a goal reach is not automatically an order. If Direct client access is incomplete, label spend/economics as incomplete rather than extrapolating a full PnL.

Do not substitute goal reaches for transactions or infer ROAS unless both spend and revenue are available with compatible attribution/time treatment.
